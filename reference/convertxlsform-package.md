# convertxlsform: Convert an XLSForm to a Word or PDF Paper Form

Reads an XLSForm questionnaire (as used by Open Data Kit and
KoboToolbox) and writes a formatted paper version as a Word document, or
as a PDF converted from that document with LibreOffice.
