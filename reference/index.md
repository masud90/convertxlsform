# Package index

## Convert

Convert an XLSForm to a Word document, or to PDF, from R or the
terminal.

- [`convertxlsform()`](https://masud90.github.io/convertxlsform/reference/convertxlsform.md)
  : Convert an XLSForm to a Word or PDF document
- [`cli()`](https://masud90.github.io/convertxlsform/reference/cli.md) :
  Convert an XLSForm from the terminal

## Inspect

Check the languages and the numbered structure of a form before
converting it.

- [`xlsform_languages()`](https://masud90.github.io/convertxlsform/reference/xlsform_languages.md)
  : List the languages of an XLSForm
- [`xlsform_outline()`](https://masud90.github.io/convertxlsform/reference/xlsform_outline.md)
  : Outline of an XLSForm

## PDF

Convert a Word document to PDF with LibreOffice or Microsoft Word.

- [`has_libreoffice()`](https://masud90.github.io/convertxlsform/reference/has_libreoffice.md)
  : Is LibreOffice available?
- [`docx_to_pdf()`](https://masud90.github.io/convertxlsform/reference/docx_to_pdf.md)
  : Convert a Word document to PDF
